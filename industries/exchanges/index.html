The challenges

The market is fast. The participant paperwork isn't.

Participants judge a venue by how easy it is to join, stay compliant and get answers — not by the matching engine alone.

  1. Membership onboarding takes weeks

    Applications, documents and approvals move by email, and neither side can see where the application stands.

  2. Conformance is a folder of PDFs

    Connectivity tests, certifications and sign-offs are filed as documents — with no workflow to chase what's missing or expired.

  3. Fee schedules live in a spreadsheet

    Participant fees, tiers and billing periods are calculated by hand, and disputes take weeks to resolve.

  4. Participant service has no memory

    Support requests, incidents and follow-ups are scattered, so every conversation starts from zero.

In practice

Join, certify, trade, settle — visibly.

Participants see their own status; your team sees every participant. Nothing hides in an inbox.

  1. 01

    A new member is onboarded

    The applicant completes the membership journey online, uploads documents, and your team approves each stage in a queue — with the applicant seeing progress throughout.

  2. 02

    A conformance check is renewed

    Connectivity certification is tracked as a workflow with expiry dates, so renewals are chased before they lapse — not discovered after.

  3. 03

    A fee dispute is settled from the record

    The fee schedule, the billing period and the underlying activity are all on the participant record, so the dispute is answered from evidence.
