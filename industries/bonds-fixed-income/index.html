The challenges

Fixed income is a process business. Clients deserve to see the process.

When the client can't see what happened to their order, the desk spends its day explaining — instead of serving.

  1. 01

    Clients can't see their own orders

    An order is placed, then silence. The client calls to ask where it is — and the desk has to ask operations.

  2. 02

    Allocations are explained by phone

    Partial fills and allocation decisions are communicated one call at a time, with no written record the client can revisit.

  3. 03

    Coupons and corporate actions surprise everyone

    Payments and actions are tracked in spreadsheets, so clients hear about them late — or not at all.

  4. 04

    Suitability is a form, not a record

    Fixed-income suitability is captured once and filed away, instead of living on the client record where the next order can check it.

In practice

The order, the coupon, the action.
