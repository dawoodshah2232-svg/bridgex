The challenges

The fund grows. The paperwork grows faster.

Every new investor multiplies the subscriptions, statements, calls and fees — unless one system carries them all.

  1. 01

    Subscriptions arrive as email attachments

    Subscription forms, KYC documents and signatures land in inboxes, and nobody can say which investor is at which stage.

  2. 02

    Investors call for what the portal should show

    Positions, statements and documents are requested by email because there is no single place an investor can see them.

  3. 03

    Capital calls and payouts are manual

    Calls and distributions are coordinated across spreadsheets and banking portals, with reconciliation afterwards — if it happens at all.

  4. 04

    Distributor trail fees nobody can reconcile

    Trail fees are calculated in a spreadsheet, disputed by email, and paid late — because the underlying investor data lives in three places.

In practice

Raise, service, distribute — without the chase.

Subscriptions complete online, calls are tracked, and distributors see the same numbers as finance.

  1. 01

    An investor subscribes without the paper chase

    The investor completes onboarding online, uploads documents once, and the subscription is approved in a queue — with the whole journey visible to your team.

  2. 02

    A capital call, issued and tracked

    A call is issued to the investor list, responses and payments are tracked against it, and the position updates in the investor portal.

  3. 03

    A distributor checks their trail fees

    The distributor opens their portal, sees the same fee statement finance sees, and requests payout — no email chain, no dispute.
