The challenges

The advice is the easy part. The admin isn't.

Every hour an advisor spends assembling statements or hunting for a suitability form is an hour not spent with a client.

  1. 01

    Suitability lives in a filing cabinet

    Risk profiles and suitability assessments are captured on paper or in a form nobody can search — so reviews start from scratch every time.

  2. 02

    Statements take a week to produce

    Investor statements are assembled by hand from three systems, checked twice, and still go out late.

  3. 03

    Pooled strategies mean pooled admin

    Subscriptions, allocations and reporting for pooled strategies multiply the paperwork with every new investor.

  4. 04

    Introducer fees are a monthly argument

    Referrers are paid late or wrong because nobody agrees on the numbers — and the spreadsheet that decides is one person's private file.

In practice

A week in the advisor's workspace.

Reviews prepared in minutes, subscriptions handled in one flow, and introducers paid without the argument.

  1. 01

    A suitability review in minutes

    Before a review meeting, the advisor opens the client record: risk profile, last assessment, holdings and notes — and the updated assessment is captured in the same place.

  2. 02

    An investor joins a pooled strategy

    Subscription, allocation and fee terms are handled in one flow, and the investor sees their position and statements in the portal.

  3. 03

    An introducer is paid without the argument

    Fees accrue on the ledger as the rules define, the introducer sees the same statement as finance, and the payout is requested and settled in the portal.
