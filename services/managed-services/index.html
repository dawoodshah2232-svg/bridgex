Services — Managed Services

Go-live is the beginning, not the finish line.

After launch, managed services keep the platform healthy and your team supported: a clear support route, monitoring that surfaces problems early, disciplined change management, and regular reviews that keep the configuration aligned with your business.

Discuss managed servicesImplementation method

The service

Four things, done steadily.

Managed services is not a hotline. It is a standing arrangement that covers support, monitoring, change and improvement — so the platform you launched is still the platform you need a year later.

Support workflows

A clear route for your team when something is wrong or unclear — triaged, tracked and closed with you, not into a void.

  • Named support channel for your team
  • Triage by impact, not by arrival order
  • Every request tracked to a resolution

Monitoring

The platform is watched so problems surface before your team has to report them.

  • Platform health and availability monitoring
  • Integration and job-failure alerts
  • Regular review of what the monitoring shows

Incident & change management

Incidents get a structured response; changes get a structured path. Neither is improvised.

  • Incident triage, response and post-incident review
  • Changes planned, tested and approved before release
  • Configuration changes versioned and reversible

Continuous optimisation

Your configuration should keep pace with your business. We review it with you and tune what no longer fits.

  • Regular configuration reviews
  • Workflow and journey refinement
  • Recommendations based on how you actually use the system

Support workflow

From “something is wrong” to resolved.

Every request follows the same path. Your team always knows where their request stands — and nothing is closed until you agree it is done.

  1. 01

    Raise

    Your team contacts the named support route with the issue.

  2. 02

    Triage

    We assess impact first — urgent operational issues jump the queue.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    The fix or answer, with you kept informed while it is in progress.

  4. 04

    Close & learn

    Confirmed with you, then recorded so the next time is faster.

Reporting

Reported from real data. Nothing else.

We report what we measure. Where the platform is instrumented, you see real availability and support figures. Where it is not, we say so — we do not publish numbers we cannot stand behind.

Uptime reporting, where instrumented

Where the platform is instrumented, we report availability from real monitoring data. We do not publish uptime figures for systems we do not measure.

Support summary

A periodic summary of requests raised, resolved and outstanding — so you can see what your team is asking for and how quickly it is answered.

Change log

A record of configuration and integration changes made on your behalf, with who approved them and when.

No invented SLAs. Support scope, response targets and reporting are agreed per engagement and written into your services agreement. Ask us and we will put the specifics in writing for your business.
