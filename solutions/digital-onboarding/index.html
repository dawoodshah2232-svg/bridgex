Solutions · Digital Onboarding & KYC

From first form to funded account, in one journey.

BridgeX Digital Onboarding guides each applicant through the steps that apply to them — and only those steps. Verification, screening and approval happen in the same flow, so good applicants reach an account quickly and risky ones reach a human.

The problem

Onboarding that treats everyone like a suspect loses the good clients first

One rigid form for every applicant: individuals answer corporate questions, low-risk clients wait behind high-risk reviews, and documents arrive by email — or not at all.

  1. 01One form for everyoneIndividuals, companies and professionals all squeezed through the same steps, most of which don't apply.

  3. 03Reviews with no queueApplications pile up in a shared mailbox; nobody knows what is waiting, or for how long.
  4. 04The handoff gapApproved applicants wait while someone manually creates their trading account — sometimes for days.

How it flows

Six steps from application to account

The applicant sees a short, clear journey. Behind it, verification, screening and approval run in sequence — or in parallel where the rules allow.

  1. 1

    Apply

    The applicant chooses a journey and completes the adaptive steps that apply to them.

  2. 2

    Verify

    Identity documents are checked through the verification adapter; contact channels are confirmed.

  3. 3

    Screen

    AML screening and suitability run against the application; flags route to a case.

  4. 4

    Approve

    Compliance reviews the queue — approving, requesting more, or escalating.

  5. 5

    Create

    Approval hands off to trading-account creation, with the record already complete.

  6. 6

    Welcome

    The new client is welcomed with their account details and next steps.

Capabilities

Journeys that adapt to the applicant

The steps an applicant sees depend on who they are, where they are and what they are applying for. Everything else stays out of the way.

Journeys

The right steps for each applicant, in the right order.

  • Individual, corporate & professional

    Three journey families, each with its own steps, documents and rules.

  • Adaptive form steps

    Later steps change based on earlier answers — country, entity type, product and risk.

  • Country & product rules

    Requirements driven by jurisdiction and product, configured rather than hard-coded.

Verification

Identity and risk checked in the flow, not after it.

  • Document upload

    Structured upload for ID, proof of address and corporate documents, with status per document.

  • ID verification adapter

    Identity checks through a verification provider adapter, with results on the application.

  • AML screening

    Screening against sanctions and watchlists through an AML integration, with flagged cases routed for review.

  • Beneficial ownership

    Corporate journeys collect and map beneficial owners, with per-owner verification status.

Completion

From verified applicant to active client, without the manual gap.

  • Suitability assessment

    Experience and suitability questions scored against product rules.

  • Email & phone verification

    Contact channels verified in the journey, before approval.

  • Approval queues & cases

    Applications route to compliance queues, with case handling for anything that needs a human.

  • Account-creation handoff

    Approved applications hand off to trading-account creation — the applicant is not left waiting.

Why it matters

Faster for good applicants, stricter where it counts

  • Benefit 1: Higher completion

    Applicants only answer what applies to them, so fewer abandon the form halfway.

  • Benefit 2: Faster time to account

    Verification and screening run in the flow, and approval hands straight to account creation.

  • Benefit 3: Consistent decisions

    Country and product rules apply the same way to every applicant — no desk-by-desk variation.

  • Benefit 4: Corporate, handled properly

    Beneficial ownership collected and mapped, instead of chased by email.

  • Benefit 5: A queue, not a mailbox

    Compliance sees every pending application, its age and its flags, in one place.

  • Benefit 6: Evidence for every approval

    Documents, checks and decisions logged on the application, ready for review.

Security & permissions

Personal data, handled with care

Onboarding collects the most sensitive data a business holds. Consent, retention and access are part of the journey design.

  • Consent & retention

    Consent captured in the journey, and retention rules applied to documents and application data.

  • Role-limited review

    Compliance sees applications; other roles see only the status they need.

  • Document handling

    Uploads stored as application documents with status and access control — not in inboxes.

  • Audit on every decision

    Approvals, rejections and escalations logged with who, when and why.

Integration approach

Connected, not isolated

Verification, screening and account creation are external systems. BridgeX orchestrates them around one application record.

  • ID verification providers

    Identity and document checks through a provider adapter; results flow back onto the application.

  • AML screening

    Sanctions and watchlist screening integrated, with flagged cases routed to review.

  • Trading platforms

    Account-creation handoff passes the approved record to the platform.

  • Financial CRM

    The applicant becomes a contact on approval, with the whole journey as history.
