The problem

Server changes are high-stakes, low-ceremony work

A leverage change touches thousands of accounts. A holiday schedule touches every symbol. Done by hand, in a hurry, with no dry-run and no second approver, a small mistake becomes a firm-wide incident — discovered by clients first.

  1. 01Changes made blindBatch updates applied directly, with no preview of what will actually change.
  2. 02One person, full powerA single operator can change leverage or symbols with no review step in between.
  3. 03No way backWhen a change goes wrong, there is no plan for rolling it back — only damage control.
  4. 04No record of the changeWho changed what, and why, lives in memory — until the audit asks.

How it flows

Six gates between an idea and a live change

The workflow is deliberately unhurried. Speed comes from batching and clarity — not from skipping checks.

  1. 1

    Draft

    The operator defines the change and its scope: accounts, groups or symbols.

  2. 2

    Validate

    Rules check the change against product, risk and schedule constraints.

  3. 3

    Dry-run

    A full preview shows every account and value that would change — no surprises.

  4. 4

    Approve

    A second authorised approver reviews the dry-run and signs off.

  5. 5

    Execute

    The job runs with live progress, per-item results and error handling.

  6. 6

    Audit

    The completed job is logged with its inputs, approvers and outcomes, exportable for review.

Capabilities

Operations with a safety net

Changes

The routine work, done in batches instead of one by one.

  • Batch updates

    Apply the same change across many accounts or symbols in one job, with per-item results.

  • Leverage changes

    Leverage updates by group or account, validated against product and risk rules.

  • Swap, session & holiday schedules

    Trading sessions, swap settings and holiday calendars managed as schedules, not surprises.

Control & evidence

The steps that turn a change into a defensible decision.

  • Validation & dry-run

    Every job validates first and previews its full effect before anything is applied.

  • Four-eyes review

    A second authorised approver reviews material changes before execution.

  • Rollback & audit logs

    Rollback where technically possible, job progress throughout, and downloadable audit logs after.

Why it matters

Fewer incidents, calmer operations

  • Benefit 1: Mistakes caught before they land

    Validation and dry-run surface bad scope and bad values while they are still free to fix.

  • Benefit 2: No single point of failure

    Four-eyes review means no one person can push a material change alone.

  • Benefit 3: Batch work, done once

    Hundreds of accounts updated in one job, with per-item results instead of per-account clicking.

  • Benefit 4: Schedules that don't surprise

    Sessions, swaps and holidays planned as calendars, visible to everyone who needs them.

  • Benefit 5: An answer for the audit

    Every job logged with who drafted, who approved and what changed — downloadable on demand.

  • Benefit 6: Rollback when it matters

    Where the platform allows it, changes can be reversed instead of repaired by hand.

Security & permissions

Role scoping is the product

TradeOps exists because server access is dangerous. The controls are the point — not a feature bolted on afterwards.

  • Secure role scoping

    Operators see and change only the servers, groups and actions their role permits.

  • Maker-checker approval

    The person who drafts a change cannot be the person who approves it.

  • No live access from this site

    The marketing website never connects to a trade server; operations run only in authorised environments.

  • Immutable job records

    Job inputs, approvals, progress and outcomes logged and downloadable.

Integration approach

Connected, not isolated

TradeOps sits between the operations team and the servers, with the CRM and finance flows on either side.

  • MT4/MT5 manager APIs

    Server operations through authorised manager APIs, in controlled environments only.

  • Financial CRM

    Account changes reflected on the contact record, with the job behind them.

  • Payments

    Funding requests route through the same approval and audit path as other money movement.

  • Notifications & reporting

    Job completion, failures and schedule changes reported to the right desks.
