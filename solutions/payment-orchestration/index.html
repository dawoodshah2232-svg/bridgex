The problem

One provider, one point of failure

When deposits run through a single gateway, every outage is your outage. Clients see failed payments, support sees the queue, and finance spends the week matching statements by hand.

  1. 01Declines with no second chanceA failed card attempt ends the journey — even when another route would have succeeded.
  2. 02No visibility into a paymentClients ask where their money is, and the honest answer is a shrug and a support ticket.
  3. 03Manual reconciliationPSP statements, CRM records and platform balances matched in spreadsheets, days after the fact.
  4. 04Withdrawals without controlPayouts approved by email thread, with no consistent record of who approved what.

Capabilities

Routing, resilience and reconciliation — by design

Orchestration means the business decides how money should move, and the system carries it out the same way every time — with a trace to prove it.

Routing

The right provider for each payment, chosen by rule — not by habit.

  • Provider-agnostic routing

    Payment providers plug in behind one abstraction, so no single provider owns your funding.

  • Method categories

    Cards, bank transfers and alternative methods, each with its own rules and availability.

  • Priority & weighted routing

    Order providers by preference, or split volume by weight across several at once.

  • Restrictions

    Country, currency, amount and client-profile rules decide which routes a payment may use.

Resilience

A decline is a detour, not a dead end.

  • Cascading retry

    After a decline, the payment automatically retries through the next eligible provider — within limits you set.

  • Payment status trace

    Every attempt, response and state change on one timeline, visible to support and finance.

  • Gateway failover

    Provider outages shift traffic to healthy routes instead of failing every payment in the queue.

  • Idempotent processing

    Retries and duplicate webhooks cannot double-charge or double-credit a payment.

Money flows & control

Deposits and withdrawals with approval, matching and evidence.

  • Deposits & withdrawals

    Both directions through the same routing and tracing, with approval steps on withdrawals.

  • Three-way reconciliation

    Provider records matched against CRM transactions and trading-platform balances.

  • Exception queue

    Unmatched or unusual items land in a queue with context — not in a spreadsheet at month end.

  • Reporting & exports

    Volumes, success rates and settlement reports, exportable for finance and audit.

Why it matters

Funding that behaves like infrastructure

  • Benefit 1: Higher completion, fewer lost deposits

    Cascading retry gives a declined payment another route instead of another lost client.

  • Benefit 2: No single-provider dependence

    Providers are interchangeable behind one abstraction, so an outage or a contract change is not a crisis.

  • Benefit 3: Answers in seconds, not tickets

    The status trace shows support exactly where a payment is, and what happened at each step.

  • Benefit 4: Controlled withdrawals

    Approval steps and an audit trail on every payout, instead of decisions made in email.

  • Benefit 5: Reconciliation without the spreadsheet

    Three-way matching runs continuously, and only the exceptions need a human.

  • Benefit 6: Rules the business can change

    Routing weights, restrictions and retry limits are configuration, not code releases.

How it flows

One payment, six checkpoints

Every movement follows the same path, whether it is a first deposit or a withdrawal after approval.

  1. 1

    Request

    The client requests a deposit or withdrawal; restrictions check country, currency, amount and profile.

  2. 2

    Route

    Priority or weighted rules select the provider and method for this payment.

  3. 3

    Attempt

    The payment is attempted; a decline cascades to the next eligible route.

  4. 4

    Confirm

    Provider confirmation is matched to the request, and the status trace is updated.

  5. 5

    Approve

    Withdrawals pass the approval step before release; deposits credit on confirmation.

  6. 6

    Reconcile

    Provider, CRM and platform records are matched; exceptions queue for review.

Security & permissions

Money movement, handled carefully

BridgeX never stores raw card data. Payments run through provider-hosted flows, and every financial action carries approval and audit.

Control by design

  • No card data stored

    Card details stay with the payment provider, in provider-hosted flows built for that purpose.

  • Approval on withdrawals

    Payouts require approval before release, with the approver and the decision recorded.

  • Signed webhooks & idempotency

    Provider callbacks are verified, and duplicate events cannot move money twice.

  • Full audit trail

    Every routing decision, attempt, approval and reconciliation action is logged.
