The problem

Growth outruns the spreadsheet — then the spreadsheet fights back

A brokerage's client base grows faster than its tooling. Leads land in inboxes, client history lives in chat threads, and nobody can say with confidence who owns a relationship — or what was promised to them.

  1. 01Leads with no ownerEnquiries arrive from the website, events and partners, then sit unassigned — or get chased by two desks at once.
  2. 02No single client viewAccounts, documents, tickets and calls are scattered across tools, so every conversation starts from zero.
  3. 03Duplicates everywhereThe same person appears three times under three spellings, and reporting quietly double-counts them.
  4. 04Nothing is auditableWhen a client disputes what was said, there is no reliable record of who changed what, and when.

Capabilities

Built around how a financial business actually sells and serves

The CRM models the relationships a brokerage really has — people, companies, accounts, desks and cases — instead of forcing them into a generic sales tool.

Leads & pipelines

From first enquiry to funded client, with ownership at every step.

  • Lead capture & pipelines

    Leads from every channel land in configurable pipelines with stages, owners and next actions.

  • Country & desk routing

    Route by country, language, product or desk, so the right team picks up every lead.

  • Duplicate management

    Matching rules flag likely duplicates at capture, and merge keeps one clean record with full history.

  • Ownership & reassignment

    Clear owners, cover rules and reassignment history — no lead is ever nobody's job.

Contact 360

One record per relationship, holding everything the team needs.

  • Individual & corporate entities

    People and companies as first-class records, linked through roles — director, trader, authorised contact, beneficiary.

  • Activities, notes & tasks

    Calls, emails, meetings and follow-ups on a single timeline, with tasks that carry owners and due dates.

  • Documents & account links

    Documents sit on the contact, and trading accounts link straight through to the platforms behind them.

  • Support cases

    Client cases open against the contact, so support sees the relationship — not just the ticket.

Control & insight

Configurable where it should be, controlled where it must be.

  • Custom fields & objects

    Add the fields and record types your business needs, without waiting for a development cycle.

  • KYC & suitability flags

    Verification and suitability status visible on the record, driving what the team can and cannot do next.

  • Role- & field-level access

    Permissions down to the field: who sees a document, who edits a status, who sees nothing at all.

  • Audit history & reporting

    Every change logged with who and when, and reporting that draws on the same single source.

How it flows

From enquiry to long-term client

Six stages, one record, no re-keying — the lead becomes the contact, and the contact keeps its whole history.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Leads arrive from web, events, partners and referrals, deduplicated on entry.

  2. 2

    Route

    Country, language and product rules send each lead to the right desk and owner.

  3. 3

    Qualify

    Calls, notes and tasks build the picture; suitability and KYC flags appear early.

  4. 4

    Convert

    The lead becomes a contact, linked to accounts — history intact, nothing re-typed.

  5. 5

    Serve

    Cases, documents and activities accumulate on the same 360 record.

  6. 6

    Report

    Pipeline, conversion and desk activity reported from the same clean data.

Why it matters

What changes when the record is right

  • Benefit 1: One source of truth

    Sales, support and compliance read the same record, so the client gets one coherent experience.

  • Benefit 2: Faster first response

    Routing and ownership mean leads reach a person quickly, instead of ageing in a shared inbox.

  • Benefit 3: Cleaner data, better decisions

    Deduplication and structured fields make pipeline and conversion reporting worth trusting.

  • Benefit 4: Corporate relationships, modelled properly

    Companies, directors and beneficiaries linked as they really relate — not flattened into notes.

  • Benefit 5: Access that matches responsibility

    Field-level permissions keep sensitive data with the roles that need it, and away from those that don't.

  • Benefit 6: An answer for every question

    Audit history means disputes, handovers and reviews start from evidence, not memory.

Security & permissions

Access, audit and control

A CRM holds the business's most sensitive relationships. BridgeX treats access as a design decision, not an afterthought.

  • Role- & field-level permissions

    Read, edit and export rights set per role, down to individual fields.

  • Full audit history

    Creates, edits, merges and deletions logged with user, time and before/after values.

  • Tenant separation

    Each organisation's data is isolated by design in the shared platform foundation.

  • KYC-driven gating

    Verification and suitability flags can restrict actions until the record is ready.

Integration approach

Connected, not isolated

The CRM is the hub other BridgeX capabilities plug into — and it talks to the systems around it through adapters, not copy-paste.

  • Trading platforms

    Account links connect the contact to their trading accounts and activity.

  • KYC & verification

    Verification status flows back onto the record from the onboarding journey.

  • Payments

    Funding activity visible alongside the relationship, with approvals in the finance flow.

  • Communications

    Email and messaging activity captured on the timeline automatically.

  • Analytics

    Pipeline, conversion and desk reporting drawn from the same live data.
