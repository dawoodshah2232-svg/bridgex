The problem

Contests are easy to announce and hard to run honestly

A leaderboard in a spreadsheet, rankings updated by hand, winners decided behind closed doors — traders notice, and the next contest gets fewer entries than the last.

  1. 01Rankings nobody trustsManual updates and unexplained changes make traders suspect the board is fixed.
  2. 02Suspicious activity, spotted lateWash trading and rule abuse found after prizes are paid — if they are found at all.
  3. 03Payouts without reviewPrizes sent before anyone checks the winner actually played by the rules.
  4. 04Campaigns that don't convertA contest runs, ends, and leaves no record of who engaged or what it was worth.

How it flows

From announcement to awarded prizes

The contest runs itself day to day; humans step in where judgement is needed — fairness review and payout approval.

  1. 1

    Create

    Format, rules, eligibility, dates and prize tiers are configured and published.

  2. 2

    Register

    Traders register; eligibility is checked and the countdown runs.

  3. 3

    Trade

    The contest goes live and rankings update from the trade feed.

  4. 4

    Rank

    Leaderboards sort and filter by format, with positions visible to participants.

  5. 5

    Review

    Suspicious activity is flagged and reviewed before standings are final.

  6. 6

    Reward

    Prizes are approved and paid out, with the full record behind the result.

Security & permissions

Fairness is a control, not a hope

A contest that pays a cheat is worse than no contest. Flags, review and approval are built into the flow.

Control by design

  • Suspicious-activity flags

    Pattern checks surface wash trading, impossible streaks and rule abuse for review.

  • Human fairness review

    Flagged entries are decided by a person, with the evidence attached — never auto-disqualified silently.

  • Payout approval

    No prize is released until the winner's standing has been reviewed and approved.

  • Full contest record

    Entries, rankings, flags, reviews and payouts logged per contest.

Integration approach

Connected, not isolated

Rankings come from the trade feed; registrations feed the CRM; prizes flow through payments. The contest is a campaign, not an island.

  • Trading feed

    Rankings computed from trading activity, so the board reflects real positions.

  • Financial CRM

    Registrations and engagement land on contact records for follow-up.

  • Payments

    Approved prizes pay out through the orchestration and approval path.

  • Partner programs

    Partner-linked campaigns attribute entries to the channel that brought them.
