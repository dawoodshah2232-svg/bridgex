Reliability & governance

Designed to be trusted.

Financial infrastructure is judged by what it refuses to let go wrong. These principles shape every BridgeX workflow — they are design decisions, stated plainly.

Role-based access

People see only what their role allows — down to individual fields. Permissions are configuration, not code, and they travel with every record.

Dual approval

Material actions need a second pair of eyes. Maker-checker is built into funding, payouts and server changes — not added as an afterthought.

Audit evidence

Every action writes an immutable record: who, what, when, and what changed. Evidence is a byproduct of doing the work, not a separate project.

Tenant boundaries

Each organisation's data is separated by design — in the data model, in the API, in every query. Boundaries are enforced, not assumed.

Safe configuration

Changes go through draft, review and publish — with rollback. Nothing reaches production by accident, and nothing changes without a record of who approved it.

These are design principles of the platform, not audit claims. BridgeX claims no certification, licence or regulatory status unless it has been independently obtained — and any such claim will name its issuer and scope.
