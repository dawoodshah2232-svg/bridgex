# BridgeX — PRD

## Product
BridgeX is a premium B2B marketing website for a capital-markets technology
brand: connected infrastructure for modern financial businesses. It presents
the platform (4 layers), 7 solutions, 9 industries, services, resources and
company pages, and converts visitors toward a private demo.

## Audience
Decision-makers and operators at multi-asset brokers, banks, securities and
derivatives firms, wealth managers, asset/fund managers, exchanges, fixed
income desks, prop firms and digital-asset firms.

## Brand promise
"Connected infrastructure for modern financial businesses."
Tagline: "Where Markets Connect."
Hero headline: "Connect every part of your financial business."

## Scope — Batch 1 (this document's baseline)
- Foundation: design tokens, self-hosted fonts, original logo system.
- Route registry: all 44 routes (home + 8 platform + 7 solutions + 9
  industries + 4 services + 7 resources + 4 company + book-demo + 3 legal).
- Global chrome: sticky header with mega-menus, mobile drill-down nav,
  footer sitemap, breadcrumbs, skip link.
- Shared components: Reveal, SectionIntro, GoldLineDivider, CtaBand,
  FaqAccordion, buttons, Container/Section, DemoLabel.
- Homepage H01–H13 in spec order, with real photography and interactive
  demo preview tabs (illustrative data, clearly labelled).

## Explicitly out of scope (later batches)
- Inner routes (Batch 2), forms (Batch 4), search/cookies/SEO files
  (Batch 4), product portals/admin/client/partner workspaces or backends
  (Batch 6 — never in this phase).

## Content truth rules
- Original BridgeX copy/branding only. Never copy FYNXT text, logo or
  visuals (FYNXT is a category reference only).
- Never invent clients, testimonials, ISO certification, awards, offices,
  stats, integrations or pricing. No fake client logos.
- Product previews show illustrative sample data, always labelled.
- AI Assistance is labelled "In development" wherever it appears.
- Integrations are described by category only; no provider is claimed
  until built, tested and signed off.

## Conversion paths
Primary: Book a Private Demo → /book-demo.
Secondary: Explore Our Platform → /platform; Talk to an Expert →
/company/contact. Every CTA links to a real planned route; no dead links.

## Non-goals
- No live trading, payments, KYC or backend of any kind in the marketing
  site. No fake dashboards presented as real product screens.
- No tracking before consent (Batch 4 owns cookies/analytics).
