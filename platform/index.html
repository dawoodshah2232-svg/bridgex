Platform

Four layers. One connected business.

BridgeX is not four products bolted together. It is one platform organised as four layers — experience, operations, integrations and intelligence — all standing on the same foundation, reading from the same records. And it arrives as yours: the same platform runs white-label behind your brand, as it does for BridgeX Apps.

Why one platform

Disconnected tools tax every team.

A brokerage rarely fails for lack of software. It fails because the software does not agree with itself — and the reconciliation happens in people's heads, in spreadsheets, and in support tickets.

01

Leads in one tool, accounts in another

Sales works a CRM, operations works a back office, and nobody can say where a client actually stands without asking three people.

02

Payments that reconcile at month-end

Deposits and withdrawals are matched long after they happen, so breaks surface as surprises instead of exceptions.

03

Partners paid from spreadsheets

Commissions are calculated outside the system that holds the trades, and every payout round starts with an argument about the numbers.

04

A client experience that says someone else's name

Clients meet a generic portal while your brand — the one they chose — is nowhere in the room.

The platform

Four layers, each with its own job.

Select a layer to see what it does, what it holds, and where it leads. The layers are separated on purpose — so each can change without breaking the others — and connected through the foundation, so nothing is ever copied between them.

Layer 01 — Experience

What clients, partners and advisors touch

The experience layer is everything a person outside your operations team sees: the client room where clients verify, fund and manage accounts; the partner portal where introducers track referrals and earnings; and the advisor interface where relationship managers work their book. Every surface carries your brand and your rules, and every action writes back to the same record the operations team sees.

  • White-label client rooms with your logo, colours and domain
  • Partner portals with referral links and earnings views
  • Advisor interfaces shaped around a client book, not a ticket queue
  • Responsive layouts that work on a phone first, not as an afterthought

Cross-cutting model

The foundation every layer stands on.

Six capabilities run under everything in BridgeX. They are why a change made in one layer is visible to the others immediately — and why a permission set once applies everywhere.

Tenant separation

Each organisation's data is kept apart by design, not by convention.

Shared data model

How it connects

One client, one journey, four layers working together.

Follow a single client from first registration to a funded, growing relationship — and notice how many systems never get involved, because the layers share the record.

  1. 01

    Register

    A client registers in your branded room. The record is created once, in the shared data model.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Documents and checks run through the operations layer, with the case and its evidence in one place.

  3. 03

    Fund

    The wallet is funded through the integrations layer; the payment reconciles against the same record.

  4. 04

    Grow

    Partners earn, dashboards report and the intelligence layer shows what happened — from the same source.

One record

The same record, at every step.

At every step, the same record is being read and written: the client room shows it, operations works it, integrations move money against it, and intelligence reports on it. There is no nightly sync to drift, and no second copy of the truth.
