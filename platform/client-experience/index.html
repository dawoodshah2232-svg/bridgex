Staying in touch

Notifications and documents, where they belong.

Clients do not chase updates when updates come to them — and documents do not get lost when they live on the record instead of in an inbox.

Notifications with a reason

Every notification is tied to a status change the client cares about: a document approved, a payment settled, a reply from support. Clients choose their channels, and every message is logged on the record — so support can see exactly what a client was told, and when.

  • Email, SMS and push — per client preference
  • Status changes, not marketing noise
  • Every send logged against the client record

Documents on the record

Identity documents, agreements, statements and uploads live against the client, the case or the account they belong to — versioned, permissioned and retrievable. When a document expires, the client is asked for a new one before it becomes a problem.

  • Versioned, so the signed copy is the retrievable copy
  • Expiry reminders before a document lapses
  • Access governed by role, like everything else

Branded journeys

A journey is a story you tell in your own words.

Registration, verification, funding and beyond — each journey is a sequence of steps with your copy, your brand and your rules. The studio is where those journeys are shaped; this is what they feel like from the client's side.

  1. 01

    Welcome in your words

    The first email, the first screen and the first step all carry your copy, your tone and your brand.

  2. 02

    Steps that adapt

    Country, client type and product shape the journey — a corporate applicant sees beneficial-owner steps; an individual does not.

  3. 03

    Progress, always visible

    Clients see what is done, what is outstanding and what happens next — at every step, on every device.

  4. 04

    A human, one tap away

    Every journey ends at a person when it needs to: a case, a message, a call-back — with the journey's context attached.

Journeys are configured in the No-Code Studio and run through the operations layer — so a journey step that needs a human lands in a queue with the client’s whole history attached, and the onboarding solution describes the verification detail.
