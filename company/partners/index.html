How partnerships work.

Whichever route fits, the shape is the same: a real conversation first, terms agreed in writing, and attribution that starts from first contact.

Joint go-to-market

For firms whose clients need brokerage operations technology — introduce BridgeX, and we work the opportunity together.

  • Shared opportunity plans
  • Joint demos and collateral
  • Clear referral terms

Technology integrations

For providers of trading platforms, payments, KYC, market data or communications — build an adapter with us.

  • Adapter architecture guidance
  • Sandbox and test access
  • Joint release notes

Referral inquiries

For introducers and consultants who spot a fit — a straightforward referral route with honest terms.

  • Simple referral process
  • Attribution from first contact

Tell us about the opportunity.

A few lines is enough to start. Tell us what your company does, who your clients are, and what working together might look like.

What happens after you apply

  1. 1. A person reads your application — not an autoresponder.
  2. 2. We reply within a few working days to arrange a conversation.
  3. 3. If there is a fit, terms are agreed per relationship, in writing.

Partner application

We do not publish tier tables or commission rates — the right terms depend on what each side brings, and we agree them together.

Your application is stored locally on this device until online submission is connected. Nothing is sent anywhere yet.
