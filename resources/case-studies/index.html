How we publish

Permission first, facts second, publication last

The process every BridgeX case study will go through — so that when the first one appears, you know exactly what stands behind it.

  1. 1

    The customer agrees

    No story starts without the customer's say-so. They choose what is on the record and what stays private, before a word is drafted.

  2. 2

    We write it together

    The draft is built from the customer's own account of what changed — in their words, about their team, with their context intact.

  3. 3

    Every fact is checked

    Numbers appear only where the customer has agreed them and we can stand behind them. Anything unverified stays out.

  4. 4

    They approve the final wording

    Nothing is published until the customer has read the finished piece and approved it. They can withdraw it afterwards, too.
